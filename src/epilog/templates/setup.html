Setup takes 10–20 min

Get a daily email with the Instagram feed you care about.

Epilog reads Instagram posts through its official API and emails your feed to you. It will run on this computer only.

What you'll need
  1. Your Instagram account, switched to Creator or Business
  2. A Facebook account, to create a Page and a free Meta developer app (step-by-step instructions are next)
  3. Gmail with 2-Step Verification

Setup remembers your progress. You can close this page and pick up where you left off.

Before you startabout 15 min

Three things to set up on Instagram and Facebook.

Instagram only allows apps to read posts through a professional account linked to a Facebook Page. Tick each one off as you go; this page remembers them.

Instagram app

Switch to a professional account. Creator or Business both work — it's free, and you can switch back later.

In the Instagram app:SettingsAccount type and toolsSwitch to professional account

Facebook Page

Create a Page. This page will be a placeholder nobody sees. Name it anything — “My Epilog” works — and pick any category, like Personal blog. Then link your Instagram to it.

In the Page's settings:Linked accountsInstagramConnect account

Meta developer app
  1. Create app, then choose the use case “Manage messaging & content on Instagram”
  2. Customize → API setup with Facebook login, then click Add required content permissions
  3. Under Permissions and features, also add instagram_manage_insights
  4. Leave it in Development mode. That's all a personal tool needs.

Gmail setup

Epilog emails you from your own Gmail to yourself, and reads your replies so you can add accounts by email. It uses an app password: a separate password just for Epilog that you can revoke anytime.

Turn on 2-Step Verificationskip if it's on

Google only offers app passwords when 2-Step Verification is on.

2-Step Verification ↗
Create an app password

Name it Epilog and copy the 16-letter code. Make sure you're signed in to the Google account you want to use.

Every day

When should your email arrive?

Epilog runs on this computer, not on a server somewhere, so it needs to be awake at that time. If it's asleep, the email arrives when you next wake it.

Add accountsone per line

Handles, @handles, or profile links all work. Only Business and Creator accounts can be read (most brands, shops, artists and publications). Personal accounts that can't be read will be noted in the footer.

Or do it by email

Get a welcome email and reply with the handles instead. Handy for adding accounts from your phone.
