Three things to set up on Instagram and Facebook

Instagram only allows apps to read posts through a professional account linked to a Facebook Page. Tick each one off as you go; this page remembers them.

Instagram app

Switch to a professional account. Creator or Business both work, and it's free. Your account needs to stay professional for Epilog to keep reading Instagram — switching back to personal stops your epilogs until you switch again.

In your Instagram account:SettingsAccount type and toolsChoose Creator or Business

Facebook Page

Create a Page. This page will be a placeholder nobody sees. Name it anything (“My Epilog” works) and pick any category, like Personal blog.

Meta developer app
  1. Go to Meta for Developers and open an account. Typically a Get started button or something similar.
    Open Meta for Developers ↗
  2. Create app, then under All use cases choose Manage messaging & content on Instagram
  3. In your new app go to Customize and choose API setup with Facebook login, then click Add required content permissions (this automatically adds 5 permissions we'll need).
  4. The button will change to Permissions and features — click it, or find Permissions and features in the left menu — then add one more permission: instagram_manage_insights
  5. No need to publish. Leave it in Development mode; that's all a personal tool needs.

Gmail setup

Epilog emails you from your own Gmail to yourself, and reads your replies so you can add accounts by email. It uses an app password: a separate password just for Epilog that you can revoke anytime.

Turn on 2-Step Verificationskip if it's on

Google only offers app passwords when 2-Step Verification is on.

Your everyday epilog

When should your epilog arrive?

Epilog runs on this computer, not on a server somewhere, so it needs to be awake at that time. If it's asleep, your epilog arrives when you next wake it.

Add accountsone per line

Handles, @handles, or profile links all work. Only Business and Creator accounts can be read (most brands, shops, artists and publications). You can dump all the accounts you're interested in, we'll let you know which personal accounts can't be read.

Or do it by email

We've emailed you a welcome note — reply to it with handles any time, which is handy from your phone. You can do both. Later on, replying to any epilog does the same thing, and “remove @handle” drops one.
