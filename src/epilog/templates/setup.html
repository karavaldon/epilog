Before you startabout 15 min

Three things to set up on Instagram and Facebook.

Instagram only allows apps to read posts through a professional account linked to a Facebook Page. Tick each one off as you go; this page remembers them.

A · Instagram app

Switch to a professional account: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account. Creator or Business both work. It's free and you can switch back later.

B · Facebook Page

Create a Page — a placeholder nobody sees is fine. Then, in the Page's settings: Linked accounts → Instagram → Connect account.

Create a Page ↗
C · Meta developer app
  1. Create app, then choose the use case “Manage messaging & content on Instagram”
  2. Customize → API setup with Facebook login, then click Add required content permissions
  3. Under Permissions and features, also add instagram_manage_insights
  4. Leave it in Development mode. That's all a personal tool needs.

Why Gmail?

Epilog emails you from your own Gmail to yourself, and reads your replies so you can add accounts by email. It uses an app password: a separate password just for Epilog that you can revoke anytime.

1 · Turn on 2-Step Verificationskip if it's on

Google only offers app passwords when 2-Step Verification is on.

2-Step Verification ↗
2 · Create an app password

Name it Epilog and copy the 16-letter code. Make sure you're signed in to the Google account you want to use.

When should your digest arrive?

Your computer needs to be awake at that time. If it's asleep, the digest arrives when it wakes.

Add accountsone per line

Handles, @handles or profile links all work. Only Business and Creator accounts can be read (most brands, shops, artists and publications). Personal accounts will be flagged.

Or do it by email

Get a welcome email and reply with the handles instead. Handy for adding accounts from your phone.
